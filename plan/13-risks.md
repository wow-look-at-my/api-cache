# 13 — Risks

What can sink the project, how likely it is, and what the plan does about each.

| Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|---|
| **The rule surface is not readable enough**, and users write rules the way buildcache users write Lua. | medium | high: the project's reason to exist | The three shipped rules are the test (`09-tool-rules.md`); decision 2 is conditional on them. `explain` shows every rule in its own terms. A shipped rule that needs a step is logged as a missing primitive. |
| **A wrong hit ships a corrupt object** and the symptom appears at link or run time. | low with the guards, catastrophic | build correctness | Framing and salt in the key; the too-new check; the `#pragma once` guard; `.pch` content in the key; CRC on every read; the content-equal diagnostic on the remote; strict mode in CI; the mutation tests in `10-testing-and-ci.md`. |
| **The Go hit path misses the budget** on Linux and ccache stays faster by more than the runtime floor. | medium | medium: the success criterion | The budget is asserted per stage in CI from phase 1; the daemon-plus-C-client option is priced and parked (decision 5); the import-set rule is asserted by a test. |
| **Windows is 10x slower per file operation** and a design validated on Linux is wrong there. | certain, already measured | medium | One-stat miss, one-open hit, metadata inside the container, rename-aside, evictor skip-on-held; the e2e matrix runs on windows-latest from phase 1. |
| **net/http's init cost** makes the remote path unaffordable on macOS and Windows. | high | medium | Decision 6: measure `net`, hand-roll the client, or move remote I/O to the spool drain. |
| **MSVC facts drift by toolset version** (the default PDB name, response-file nesting, `/Fo` semantics all differed from the documentation). | high | medium | The cl rule is written from measured behaviour with the run recorded; the corpus and the e2e project run on the current windows-latest image on every push; `explain` makes a drift visible as an unclassified argument rather than a silent misclassification. |
| **The localized `/showIncludes` prefix** appears on a machine and every cl compile declines. | low for this user | low | Out of scope by decision; the decline is loud and names the reason. |
| **The upload spool grows** when a remote is down and drains slowly when it returns. | medium | low | Spool entries expire with `max-age`; the drain is bounded per invocation; `flush` and `stats` show the backlog; a down remote costs a 50 ms connect budget per drain attempt, backed off. |
| **binpazer needs a change** (a streaming index, a per-block name) and the format is upstream. | low | low | It is the org's own MIT repository, a submodule pinned by commit, with C and Go implementations that CI cross-checks; the directory block covers naming without a format change. |
| **go-s3-server has no LICENSE**, blocking the lift of its infrastructure. | certain today | low, administrative | Add the licence before phase 4; until then the files are reference, not code. |
| **Two rules match one invocation** after an overlay, or an overlay silently shadows a shipped row. | medium | medium | Load errors for ambiguity; `explain --rules` shows provenance per row; the resolved-form golden test in CI. |
| **A hostile or corrupt remote entry** directs a restore outside the output paths. | low | high | Roles, not paths, in the entry directory; CRC before decode; read-only credentials as the norm; provenance on the server. |
| **The daemon-free design cannot amortise a slow tool-identity query** (`rustc -vV` plus a sysroot glob per exec). | medium | medium | The identity memo keyed by `path:size:mtime` with a TTL, shared across processes through the store; measured in phase 3. |
| **The conformance corpus is thin** and "practically usable" is unproven. | high at the start | medium | Coverage is a published number; every bug report adds a case; `explain --record` captures argv from real builds; the reference builds (kernel, Chromium, Unreal) are in the phase-6 corpus plan. |
| **api-dsl's render cost regresses** because nothing upstream caches. | low | low | The consumer-local cache and shared root do not depend on upstream; the three additive proposals are optional. |
| **Scope creep** into nvcc, modules, distribution, MCP. | medium | medium | The deferred table with a reopening condition per item (`11-roadmap.md`). |
